Reps & Weights Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Last updated September 23, 2026

Your workout data stays with you.

Reps & Weights stores workout information on your device. The current version does not create accounts or send your workout records to a Reps & Weights server.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information needed to plan and record workouts, including exercises, saved workouts, completed workouts, weights, repetitions, notes, and progression recommendations. This information remains in the app's local storage unless you choose to export it.

Information we receive

We do not operate an account system, advertising service, analytics service, or cloud service that receives your workout records. We do not sell personal information.

The app does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Apple may provide us with limited app-usage or crash diagnostics under Apple's policies and your device settings. We use any diagnostics we receive only to maintain and improve the app. They are not intended to contain your workout records.

Backups and sharing

You can export a backup through your device's sharing interface. The backup is a readable, unencrypted file that may contain all of your workout information. You choose where it is sent or stored, and Reps & Weights does not receive a copy.

Once you send a backup to another person or service, that recipient's privacy and security practices apply. The app may create temporary local copies while exporting or restoring a backup. Your device manages those temporary files as part of the app's storage.

Retention, deletion, and security

Workout information remains on your device until you edit it, restore a backup, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local application data, but it does not delete files you exported or copies retained in device or cloud backups. Offloading the app may preserve its local data.

The app stores information within its private application storage and relies on the security protections provided by your device and operating system. No storage method can guarantee absolute security, so exported backups should be protected carefully.

This policy website

We do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to this website. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical information about site visits under its privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy before materially changing how the app handles information. The effective date above will change when the policy changes.
