Reps & Weights Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Last updated September 23, 2026

Your workout data stays with you.

Reps & Weights is a local-first mobile strength workout app. The current version does not create accounts or send workout information to a Reps & Weights server.

Information stored by the app

The app stores workout exercises, saved workouts, active workout state, completed workout history, weights, repetitions, notes, and progression recommendations locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's workout planning, logging, history, backup, and progression features.

Information we collect

Reps & Weights does not collect or transmit personal information or workout data to the developer. The current version has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated cloud service. We do not sell personal information.

Apple may process App Store, purchase, diagnostic, or device information under Apple's own policies. Reps & Weights does not receive your locally stored workout records from Apple.

Backups and sharing

You can export a portable JSON backup using the device's sharing interface. You choose where that file is sent or stored. A destination service, such as cloud storage or messaging, may process the exported file under its own privacy terms. Reps & Weights does not receive a copy.

Restoring a backup replaces the app's current local data after confirmation. Device or operating-system backups may also retain application data according to your device and cloud-backup settings.

Security and retention

Workout data is stored in the app's private local storage and uses iOS file protection. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so exported backup files should be stored and shared carefully.

Local data remains on your device until you edit it, restore a backup, or delete the app, subject to the device platform's backup and restore behavior.

Your choices

You can edit workout and exercise information within the app. You can export a copy of your information through the backup feature. Deleting the app removes its local application data from the device, subject to operating-system backups. Because the app has no cloud account or developer-operated server, there is no separate server-side record for us to delete.

Children's privacy

Reps & Weights is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before a version adds accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, crash reporting that transmits personal data, or other material changes to data handling. The effective date above will change when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public legal repository. Do not include workout details, exported backups, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
